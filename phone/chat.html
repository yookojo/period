Dot

Meet Dot 👋

Dot is a helper that does tasks for you on the computer. You just tell it what you want, like texting a friend.

💬
Tell it what you needType at the bottom and press Enter. For example: "find me cheap flights to Newark for Thanksgiving" or "what's the weather this weekend?"
🎤
Or just say itClick the microphone, talk, then click it again when you're done. Your words appear in the box. Press Enter to send. Dot answers in writing, never out loud.
💭
Wait for its answerWhen you see the three bouncing dots, Dot is working. Bigger jobs can take a few minutes. You can close this window and it keeps going.
✅
It asks before anything bigBefore spending money or changing important things, Dot asks first. Tap Yes or No, or just type your answer.
🖥️
Watch it workDot has its own separate computer that lives inside this Mac. Click the little screen at the top right to watch what it's doing. You can also click and type in there to help it, like signing in to a website.
❓
Need this again?Click the ? at the top left any time. And if you're ever unsure, just ask Dot. It's happy to explain.
